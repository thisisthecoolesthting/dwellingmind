# DwellingMind Copy and Brand Guide

## Identity
- Brand: DwellingMind
- Public domain: DwellingMind.app
- Tagline: Your home remembers everything.
- Positioning: The living record and intelligence system for your home.

## Voice
Calm, practical, trustworthy, and clear. Avoid robot imagery, AI hype, fear tactics, and claims that the product can physically inspect or repair a home.

## Core customer problem
Important home information is scattered across receipts, emails, drawers, photos, contractor messages, and memory. It becomes hardest to find exactly when the homeowner needs it.

## Core promise
Everything important about the home becomes organized, connected, searchable, and useful over time.

## Visual palette
- Deep navy: #0F1D2E
- Slate: #3E5066
- Sky blue: #7FB6E6
- Sage: #A7C3A1
- Warm off-white: #F6F4EF

## Recommended fonts
Sora or Manrope for headings; Inter for body copy. The included site uses system-safe Inter fallbacks and requires no external font request.
