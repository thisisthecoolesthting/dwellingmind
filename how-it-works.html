How it works

From scattered details to a living home record.

DwellingMind starts small and becomes more useful every time you add a record, complete maintenance, or improve the property.

01 — CREATE THE HOME

Add the property

Enter the basic property information, important rooms, major systems, and household contacts.

02 — CAPTURE

Add what matters

Photograph labels, upload manuals and receipts, forward documents, or enter a record manually.

03 — VERIFY

Confirm the facts

Review extracted details before they become part of the trusted home record.

04 — CONNECT

Link the history

Connect documents, warranties, maintenance, parts, contractors, and projects to the relevant equipment or room.

05 — ACT

Stay ahead

Use recurring reminders, project tracking, warranty alerts, and seasonal home checklists.

06 — ASK & EXPORT

Use the memory

Ask grounded questions or export a property report for insurance, service, sale, or family records.

The first launch workflow

Photograph a model label. DwellingMind creates an equipment record, captures the serial number, attaches the manual, suggests maintenance, and reminds you when service is due.
