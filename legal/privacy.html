Privacy Policy

Draft date: August 2, 2026

This launch draft must be reviewed and adapted before production. The current prototype stores demo records and early-access submissions in the user’s browser using localStorage. It does not transmit those records to a DwellingMind server.

Information we may collect

A future production service may collect account information, property records, uploaded documents, equipment details, usage events, support messages, and billing information. The final policy must identify the actual providers and purposes in use.

How information is used

Information should be used to provide and secure the service, organize the user’s home record, produce requested reminders and answers, process payments, communicate with the user, and improve the product.

User control

The intended product design includes access, correction, export, sharing, and deletion controls. Final retention periods and legal rights must be documented before launch.
